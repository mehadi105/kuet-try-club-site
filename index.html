What TRY does

KUET students volunteer through TRY to support education, emergency aid, seasonal relief, and on-campus community service — planned, verified, and reported with transparency.

📚

Scholarship support

Monthly education help for deserving students, with follow-up and respect for privacy.

🏥

Emergency medical aid

Verified fundraising appeals for urgent treatment, shared only through official TRY posts.

🎁

Seasonal relief

Eid gifts, food support, and targeted help so families can get through hard seasons with dignity.

🤝

Field work & drives

Distributions, blood donation camps, and community outreach coordinated by student volunteers.
